## Welcome to ANAN Core

ANAN Core is the radio's own face: an OpenHPSDR Protocol-1/2/3 client that runs *on the radio itself* (the internal Pi of a G2, or an attached Pi), on the 8-inch front touch panel, and identically on any PC pointed at the same radio. One application, everywhere — the desk, the shack PC, a browser on your LAN, a phone, and a remote session from anywhere on the internet.

This manual is the go-to reference for operating the software. It walks the appliance flow first (power on → on the air), then every part of the interface, then **every Settings menu in detail**, then remote operation, updating, and troubleshooting. The engineering record (why things are built the way they are) lives separately in the repository's `docs/` tree.
