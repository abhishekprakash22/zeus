## The desktop workspace

The desktop/browser interface puts the whole station on one screen: movable, resizable **panels** on a grid, arranged the way you operate. Four regions:

| Region | Where | What lives there |
|---|---|---|
| **Top bar** | Across the top | Brand mark + detected-radio label, the always-visible radio controls (mode, filter, band, step, FRONT-END preamp/attenuator, AGC, SQL, AF), and the connect/disconnect control |
| **Layout bar** | Down the left edge | One tab per saved layout, an **+ Add** tab, and the **Settings** gear at the bottom |
| **Workspace** | The large center | Your panels, on a grid that never scrolls — it shrinks to fit like a hardware front panel |
| **Transport bar** | Pinned across the bottom | MOX / TUN / MON / PS, drive, mic meter, secondary controls, live status chips |

**Layouts** are saved arrangements of panels, per radio model. Click a tab to switch instantly; **+ Add** creates one (label, emoji icon, description, optional *Lock panel positions*); the gear on a tab edits it; ✕ deletes (never your last one).

**Panels.** The round **+** button (bottom-right) opens the Add Panel window — a category rail (Spectrum, VFO, Meters, DSP, Log, Tools, Controls, Plugins…) and a searchable list. Panels include the Panadapter/World-Map, Frequency · VFO, S-Meter (bar, analog, edgewise), QRZ Lookup, rotator, the DSP rack, CW console and decoder, Logbook, TX meters and fidelity, filter panel, PureSignal panel, band/mode/step pickers, the WAV tape-deck recorder, meter groups (multi-instance), DX spots, chat, HamClock, space weather, and a general URL-embed panel. Drag a panel by its header strip to move (others reflow); drag the lower-right handle to resize. Panels can be detached into their own OS windows on the desktop build.

**Settings** replaces the workspace when opened (gear at the bottom of the layout bar); any layout tab, or **Esc**, brings the workspace back.
