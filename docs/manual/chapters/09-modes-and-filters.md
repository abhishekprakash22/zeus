## Modes and filters

- **Modes:** LSB, USB, CW-L/U, AM, SAM, FM, DIGL/DIGU, DRM, SPEC — with per-mode filter preset memories.
- **Filter presets:** F1…F10 plus two variable slots per mode; adjustable low/high edges. The **Bandwidth Filter** panel (a floating card in the G2 layout, one per receiver) draws the passband — drag its edges or drag the passband on the main panadapter.
- **DDC sample rate:** the spectrum width control (48–1536 kHz) sets the receiver's DDC rate — wider sees more band, narrower spends the same bits on less spectrum (DSP tab → Bandwidth).
