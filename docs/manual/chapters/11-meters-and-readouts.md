## Meters and readouts

- **S-meter:** bar, **analog moving-coil**, and edgewise styles; calibrated per board. RX1 reads the calibrated meter; RX2's flag meter is estimated from its spectrum. Flag S-meters carry S-point markings, an S readout, and a peak-hold tick.
- **TX meters:** Final Output (forward power and SWR), the **Signal Chain** staging meters (mic → EQ → leveler → CFC → compressor → out), and **Gain Reduction** (how hard the processing is working). A compact FWD / SWR / ALC readout sits inline in the G2 transport row.
- **Mic meter:** set your audio *before* keying — the mic meter runs un-keyed.
- **Two-tone IMD:** with the two-tone generator keyed, the PureSignal panel reports live **IMD3 / IMD5** measured from the TX spectrum (§12).
