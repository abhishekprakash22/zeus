## Transmitting

- **MOX** keys the radio; **TUN** emits a low-drive carrier at its own TUN power setting. **DRV** sets drive 0–100%. **MON** (amber, between TUN and PS) monitors your own processed TX audio — with MOX *off* it is a safe preview that transmits nothing; it also works over a remote link.
- **PA settings** (Settings → PA SETTINGS, §21): rated PA output, per-band PA gain, SWR protection, TX timeout, TX tail delay. This is the one *staged* tab — press APPLY.
- **PA guards:** TX drops automatically when SWR reads above 2.5:1 on MOX (6:1 on TUN) for half a second, and when a transmission runs past **TX Timeout** (default 120 s). **SWR Protection** can be switched off for a bridge reading you distrust or a rough load you match through an external tuner — with it off nothing but the timeout protects the PA, and a red "PA unguarded" tag reminds you. It is a desk-only control: a remote session cannot change it.
- **TX bandpass filter:** set your transmitted low/high edges in the TX filter panel.
- **Mic sources:** the G2's **front-panel mic is first-class** — no PC audio involved. A browser/headset mic also works (source **Host**). The TX audio ingest is *single-select* — one source, never a mix; the "mic" status chip reports whichever source is actually live. **Mic PTT** and **PTT on Tip** (tip/ring swap for non-Apache-wired mics) live in the RADIO tab and persist.
- **TX audio processing:** leveler, **CFC** (continuous frequency compressor), graphic EQ, and compressor — in the AUDIO PROC / TX Audio Tools panel with live stage meters. **TX Audio Profiles** save whole chains (ESSB / DX / Studio) and restore them in one tap; unsaved edits are flagged before you disconnect.
- **Phase rotator auto-cal (WDSP 2.1):** in the TX fidelity panel, the rotator's **Auto** box lets WDSP find the corner itself — it measures the voice waveform's positive/negative asymmetry after the rotator and walks the corner until minimized. The status line shows SEARCHING (with the current step) or CONVERGED, the live corner, and IN → OUT asymmetry; **Restart search** re-arms after a mic or processing change. With Auto on, the Corner box is read-only.
- **Two-tone generator (2TON):** keys the transmitter with the standard two-tone test (tone frequencies settable in the PureSignal tab). Its key lights TX red — it is a real transmission; dummy load or clear frequency.
- **Antenna discipline is yours:** always have an antenna or dummy load on the active port before keying.
