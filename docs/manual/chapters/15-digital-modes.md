## Digital modes

- **FT8/FT4** run natively on the radio (ZEUS DIGITAL tab installs/updates the suite): band decoding, decode history, armed auto-sequencing to work a station, QSO logging with worked-before highlighting, WSJT-X-compatible UDP logging to N1MM+/JTAlert/GridTracker, and TX macros.
- **FreeDV 700D/E** digital voice is built into the core — no external apps. **RADE V1**, the neural codec, is available as an installable modem; the FreeDV window handles install, RX, and TX.
- **WSPR:** in-core decoding plus a transmit beacon slot.
