## Keyboard and mouse shortcuts

- **Esc** — close Settings / dismiss popovers.
- **Scroll on the VFO digits** — step that decade; **click the digits** — type a frequency in kHz.
- **Scroll on the panadapter** — tune by the current step; **drag** — slide; **pinch** — zoom (touch).
- **Ctrl+Shift+R** — hard-reload the interface after an update.
- Slider fine-trim and panel-specific keys are listed in each panel's tooltip.
