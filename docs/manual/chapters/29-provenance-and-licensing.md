## Provenance and licensing

ANAN Core is a GPL work in the OpenHPSDR lineage. The FPGA keyer, Saturn register maps, p2app, and gateware come from the OpenHPSDR/Saturn community; the DSP core is WDSP (Warren Pratt NR0V), and lineage credits are listed in ATTRIBUTIONS.md and the About tab. Source for every shipped release is public in the project repository; this manual is maintained in-repo (`docs/`) and updated alongside the features it describes.
